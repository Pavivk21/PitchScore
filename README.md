# PitchScore 🚀

## AI-Powered Startup Pitch Evaluation & Investor Matching Platform

PitchScore is an AI-driven platform designed to help startup founders evaluate and improve their business pitches. It analyzes a startup idea across multiple business dimensions, provides an overall pitch score, generates feedback, and recommends suitable investors based on the startup profile.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 AI-powered startup pitch evaluation
- 🎯 Overall pitch scoring
- 💡 Strengths and weaknesses analysis
- 📈 Market and business model evaluation
- 💰 Investment readiness assessment
- 🦈 Investor/Shark matching
- 📝 Personalized pitch improvement suggestions
- 📊 Interactive analysis and visualizations

## 📋 Evaluation Criteria

PitchScore evaluates a startup based on:

- Problem & Solution
- Market Potential
- Business Model
- Innovation & USP
- Revenue Potential
- Scalability
- Competitive Advantage
- Financial Potential
- Investment Readiness

## 🚀 How It Works

1. User submits their startup pitch.
2. PitchScore analyzes the pitch based on predefined business criteria.
3. The system generates individual scores for different evaluation areas.
4. An overall pitch score is calculated.
5. The system identifies strengths and weaknesses.
6. Personalized recommendations are provided to improve the pitch.
7. Suitable investors are recommended based on the startup profile.

## 🌟 Why PitchScore?

Pitch evaluation is often subjective and depends heavily on feedback from mentors or peers. PitchScore aims to provide a structured and data-driven approach to evaluating startup pitches, helping founders identify weaknesses and improve their presentations before approaching investors.
